Runway
‘Learning to float’
+ Paris SS27
+ Louis Vuitton
+ Hermès · Dior

Louis Vuitton closed Paris Fashion Week on 6 October with floating silk trousers and pared-back tailoring. Nicolas Ghesquière called the feeling “a second skin”. Hermès and Jonathan Anderson’s Dior chased the same weightlessness.

The mood around it was loud, though. Minimalism is out, orange is the new colour, and Paris answered Milan’s “season of the skirt” with party pants: skirted silk slacks at Vuitton, sparkly snakeskin jeans at Balmain.

02 / 03
Materials
‘Old into new’
+ Munich
+ 13–14 October
+ Textile-to-textile

Performance Days, the fabric fair where sportswear and fashion brands source next season’s materials, opens in Munich tomorrow. Its headline theme is textile-to-textile: turning worn clothes back into new fibre instead of relying on recycled plastic bottles, with a Wool Forum alongside.

FashionUnited counts 520 textile-to-textile recyclers worldwide. Next stop: Tex+Vision in Seoul, 21–23 October, with bio-based fibres, vegan leather and smart fabrics.

03 / 03
AI
‘A shield for designers’
+ Paris
+ IPFC
+ Design rights

Fashion entrepreneur Thomas Cohen, formerly of Bonton and Merci, is launching IPFC with musician Emmanuel Lipszyc. It is modelled on France’s music-rights society and aims to help brands and creators protect their work from generative AI systems.

It lands as the big groups move deeper into AI: L’Oréal has announced an alliance with OpenAI, and Kering’s CEO Luca de Meo has named innovation a pillar of the group’s transformation.
